WINE DINNER PARTY IN TOKYO

Home › Blog › Wine & wineries › WINE DINNER PARTY IN TOKYO

Wine dinner party has been recently celebrated at Tokyo Wine Club. The wines from the Republic of Moldova and Romania were higly appreciated.

This event has offered a very nice possibility for the Embassy of Republic of Moldova in Japan to inform the Japanese public about the wine industry in our country.

The participants have tasted and marked high quality of our Moldovan wines presented with special attitude by Yuko Iwasaki from @Yu Corpration and Angela Albina from @Moldova Market Japan!

The team of @EnjoyMoldova is looking forward to welcome and meet the guests from Japan to show our country’s hospitality and treat them with authentic food and premium wines.

Join our tours and you’ll find more about our country, people, gastronomy, culture and traditions which are very carefully preserved by the Moldovan nation.
