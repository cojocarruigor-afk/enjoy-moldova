TOP TOURS MOLDOVA

“Two roads diverged in a wood and I – I took the one less traveled by.” – Robert Frost

Take one of the Top Tours Moldova and start exploring one of the least known countries in Europe. Lonely Planet readers voted Moldova as the number one country “off the beaten path” but since then, the country has elaborated Top Tours Moldova and nowadays becomes more and more popular and attractive for travelers all over the world.

Wine Top Tours Moldova

Visit the country and take one of the top tours Moldova like the Wine Tours and get acquainted with the wine making process and taste the exquisite wines of local and classical sorts of grapes. Take the top tours Moldova, Cricova Winery, explore the amazing and spectacular underground city by electrical train. Emerge yourself in the kingdom of Dionysus and let the world go round without you for a while.

The top tours Moldova, Milestii Mici Winery is having the richest Wine collection in the world. They will be proud to take you in the wine cellars and show the Guinness Book Certificate.

Another top tours Moldova destination is MIMI Winery that will offer the guests an unforgettable experience of wine and dine in a real ancient castle. There many wineries that can be qualified as top tours Moldova. The country is not only about wine, it is also about culture traditions and cuisine.

Another destination for top tours Moldova is The Open Air Museum Complex Orheiul Vechi. It offers the guests the experience of staying in a genuine traditional village in a house of character. One can taste traditional food and have cooking master-classes or work at the local farm.

One of the top tours Moldova destinations is Transnistria and the Transnistria Tour. It is in high demand nowadays. The self- proclaimed country situated on the border of Dniester River neighboring with Ukraine that is offering the best of old Soviet period. Together with the finest brandy’s and a mix of Moldova, Ukraine and Russian cuisines.

By visiting another top tours Moldova, The town of Soroca situated in the North of the country, travelers will get acquainted with the local Roma community and will visit the house of the local king, so-called baron. The famous Fortress of Soroca is also located in that area.

Moldova is worth discovering and our company is eager to share the top tours Moldova!
