Your local DMC since 2001

We started in Chișinău in 2001 as Solei-Turism and grew into one of the leading inbound travel companies in Moldova. We have been awarded Best Inbound Travel Agency in the Republic of Moldova more than once, most recently first place in inbound tourism at the Moldova Tourism Awards 2024.

We plan the logistics and every detail of your trip, so you can simply enjoy it. We design each tour around you and match you with the best places, people and experiences. Our guides help you understand local life, not just see the sights.

Our philosophy is simple: quality, satisfaction, integrity and efficiency. For more than 20 years we have also arranged travel for football teams and sport events.

We work with individual travellers, groups, companies and travel agencies, and present Moldova every year at international fairs such as ITB Berlin.

Company’s Profile

Founded in 2001, the company “Solei – Tourism” is a travel company that has developed considerably over the years and today occupies an important place on the tourism market of the Republic of Moldova under the brand “Enjoy Moldova by Solei Tourism” as a DMC.

During many years of fruitful activity on the tourism services market, we have accumulated considerable experience in the field of tourism, business travel and MICE services. We have established relationships with the most prominent airlines, travel companies, hotels and worldwide-recognized associations.

The perfect image of the agency, the qualified team, the staff’s professionalism, the efficient organization of the services for individual and corporate customers, serves as the basis for Enjoy Moldova by Solei Tourism to keep its position among the leaders of the tourism market of Moldova for many years.

Our company was repeatedly awarded as the Best Inbound Travel Agency in the Republic of Moldova.

Enjoy Moldova Team can boast a rich experience in handling services for football teams , being gained in the course of the last 20 years, and thus creating an excellent reputation as the leading football events arranging company in Moldova.

Guide Services

Why travelers choose Enjoy Moldova by Solei Tourism DMC?

We will handle the logistics and will take care of all the details of your trip so you can focus on enjoying the trip

We design a tour just for you and we will match you with the best places, people&experience

Our highly professional guides will make our travelers truly understand the local life and deliver authentic travel experience about Moldova

By booking a C ombined Tour with Romania or a city breaks tour, one can explore neighboring countries in a relatively short period but getting a profound look into culture, history, religion, nature and traditions. Most visited capitals are on the list (Bucharest, Iasi, Chisinau, Tiraspol)

Our “Special Event Tours” will provide you with a memorable experience about the national holidays and festivals around the year and will offer a deep insight into rich ancient traditions of the Moldovans. Wine Festival tour is the best opportunity to make acquaintance with almost all wine producers from all over Moldova, meet the wine-makers in person and undoubtedly taste best Moldovan wines.

Our Wine&Gastronomy tours offer a nice opportunity to find yourself in the world’s biggest wine cellars, try fine wines and sophisticated food from the best wineries of Moldova, have a private talk with a wine-maker and even ride a bicycle through the underground wine tunnels. What about learning about food&wine pairing and cooking classes?

Our Heritage Tours will reveal the stories of the Jews, will take you back to the USSR, will get you acquainted with the unique Gagausian nation living in Moldova, and will make you understand who were the “Bessarabian Germans”. And what about tracing the roots of Turkic and Polish roots in Moldova?

Our Mixed Activity Tours will surely make your stay active, funny and remarkable. Such programs offer a variety of interesting activities as cooking classes, wine paintings, grape picking&crushing, creating a rug or a pottery masterpiece, riding a bicycle and kayaking.

Our Countryside Tours will take the travelers to the remote areas of the capital, far from the city noise, urban bustle and close to the nature and countryside life with a special authentic experience.

Countryside tours are about overnights in rural guest houses, home-made food and wine, personal approach and rare insight into the way of life of the locals.

Our Urban Day Tours are good for those coming to Moldova on a short business trip and willing to combine work with leisure and relax. We’ll show you the capital of Moldova – Chisinau, with wide boulevards and straight streets, large green parks filled with cafes and local life, diverse architecture, including 19 th century buildings, grands Soviet architecture of white limestone, and of course modern urban designs. During such tours you can learn more about Chisinau’s history, heritage and mix of culture by visiting the national museums, churches and art galleries. To add color and diversity to urban tours, we’ll arrange wine tours and tastings to one of the wineries where you get either into the world’s biggest underground wine cities or to the oldest wine estates.
