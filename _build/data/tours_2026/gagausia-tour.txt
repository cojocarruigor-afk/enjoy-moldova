GAGAUZIA – DISCOVER THE CULTURE, TRADITIONS & HOSPITALITY OF THE GAGAUZ PEOPLE
Travel to the south of Moldova and discover the fascinating history, culture and traditions of the Gagauz people – a unique community of Turkic origin whose members traditionally follow the Orthodox Christian faith.
COMRAT – THE HEART OF GAGAUZIA
Our journey begins in Comrat, the capital of the Autonomous Territorial Unit of Gagauzia. This welcoming town is the cultural and administrative heart of the region, where Gagauz, Moldovan and Turkish influences come together.
Enjoy a walking tour through the city and discover its main landmarks, including the Cathedral of Saint John the Baptist, the Gagauz History and Ethnography Museum, Comrat State University, the Turkish Library and Victory Square.
A visit to the Central Market offers a glimpse into local everyday life, with an abundance of fresh regional produce and traditional products.
CONGAZ – GAGAUZ TRADITIONS & AUTHENTIC FLAVOURS
Continue to Congaz, widely known as one of the largest villages in Europe, where you will discover the traditional way of life and warm hospitality of the Gagauz people.
Visit the Gagauz Sofrası ethno-complex, where you will be welcomed in the traditional manner with bread and salt. Explore the traditional household; discover the local heritage museum and step inside an authentic Gagauz wine cellar, learning more about the customs and everyday life of the community.
The visit continues with a traditional Gagauz lunch, offering an opportunity to savor authentic local dishes and experience the distinctive flavors of the region. Naturally, locally produced Gagauz wine will also be part of the experience.
BEȘALMA – THE HISTORY & CULTURAL HERITAGE OF THE GAGAUZ PEOPLE
In the afternoon, travel to the nearby village of Beșalma to visit the Museum of History and Ethnography, one of the most important cultural institutions dedicated to the Gagauz people.
Founded in 1966 by writer Dimitri Kara-Coban, the museum preserves and presents the history, origins and cultural heritage of this unique Turkic-speaking Orthodox community. Its collection brings together hundreds of artifacts gathered from villages across Gagauzia, offering visitors a deeper understanding of Gagauz identity, traditions and everyday life.
To make the experience even more memorable, enjoy a traditional folk performance by local Gagauz women, featuring national songs and dances and offering a rare opportunity to hear the Gagauz language.
After a day filled with cultural discoveries, local flavors and authentic encounters, return to Chișinău.
