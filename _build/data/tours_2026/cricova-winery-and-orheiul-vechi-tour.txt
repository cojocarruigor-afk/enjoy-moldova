MOLDOVA IN A DAY: MONASTERIES, RURAL TRADITIONS & UNDERGROUND WINE CITY
A journey through Moldova's spiritual heritage, authentic village life, breathtaking landscapes and legendary wine cellars.
Discover the very best of Moldova in one unforgettable day! 
From the peaceful atmosphere of ancient monasteries and the spectacular landscapes of Old Orhei to the warm hospitality of a traditional Moldovan village and the impressive underground wine city of Cricova, this journey brings together the country's most cherished treasures.
TO CURCHI MONASTERY – A PLACE OF PEACE AND SPIRITUAL BEAUTY
Our journey begins with a visit to Curchi Monastery, one of the most beautiful and significant Orthodox monastic complexes in Moldova. Surrounded by greenery and peaceful landscapes, the monastery impresses visitors with its magnificent architecture, elegant churches and serene atmosphere. Discover its fascinating history, religious traditions and architectural heritage while enjoying a moment of tranquility away from the bustle of the city.
TO OLD ORHEI – WHERE HISTORY, NATURE AND TRADITIONS MEET
Continue to Old Orhei, one of Moldova's most remarkable cultural and archaeological destinations, famous for its spectacular limestone cliffs, ancient settlements and cave monasteries overlooking the Răut River valley.
Before exploring the complex, enjoy a traditional Moldovan lunch at a charming countryside guesthouse in Butuceni or Trebujeni village. Be welcomed with warm hospitality and homemade dishes prepared using fresh, locally sourced ingredients, accompanied by delicious homemade wine. Experience the authentic flavors of rural Moldova in a cozy and welcoming atmosphere.
After lunch, discover the fascinating open-air historical and archaeological complex of Old Orhei. Visit the ancient cave monastery carved into the limestone cliffs and learn about the monks who once lived and prayed in these remarkable rock-hewn chambers.
Explore the archaeological remains of the medieval Tatar-Mongol town, including the impressive ruins of the Tatar Baths, which reveal fascinating details about urban life and bathing traditions in the 14th-century settlement.
Our journey continues through Butuceni, a picturesque Moldovan village where traditional architecture and rural customs have been carefully preserved. Stroll along its charming streets; admire original village houses, cozy courtyards and traditional wells with drinking water.
Step inside the local village museum, housed in an authentic 19th-century peasant home, and discover how Moldovan families lived, worked and preserved their traditions through generations.
And, of course, take time to capture some unforgettable photographs! The panoramic views of the limestone cliffs, winding Răut River and spectacular valley create some of the most breathtaking landscapes in the country.
TO CRICOVA WINE CELLARS – DISCOVER THE UNDERGROUND CITY OF WINE
In the second half of the day, we continue our journey to Cricova Winery, one of Moldova's most iconic wine destinations and a true underground wonder.
Founded in 1952, Cricova is internationally renowned for its extraordinary underground galleries, stretching for more than 120 kilometres and forming an impressive subterranean city dedicated to wine production and storage.
Travel through underground streets named after famous grape varieties and discover the fascinating world hidden beneath the surface. Explore the wine cellars, learn about the winery's history and traditions, and discover how Moldova's exceptional wines and sparkling wines are produced and preserved.
The underground galleries also house an impressive National Wine Collection, including rare and historic bottles that reflect the rich heritage of winemaking.
And what would a visit to Cricova be without a wine tasting? Enjoy a selection of carefully chosen Moldovan wines and discover the aromas, flavors and character that have made Moldovan winemaking famous far beyond the country's borders.
At the end of this memorable day, we return to Chișinău, leaving you with the evening free to enjoy the city at your own pace.
A day filled with spirituality, authentic encounters, spectacular landscapes and the unforgettable taste of Moldovan wine!
