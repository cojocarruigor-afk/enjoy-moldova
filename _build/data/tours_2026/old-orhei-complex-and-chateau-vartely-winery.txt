OLD ORHEI & CHÂTEAU VARTELY: HISTORY, NATURE & THE LEGEND OF WINE
A journey through Moldova's ancient heritage, breathtaking landscapes and the fascinating world of winemaking!
Discover two remarkable destinations in the heart of Moldova! Explore the fascinating archaeological and cultural heritage of Old Orhei, admire its spectacular limestone cliffs and centuries-old cave monasteries, then continue to Château Vartely for an unforgettable wine experience combining tradition, innovation and exceptional flavours.
 VISIT TO OLD ORHEI RESERVE
Our journey begins with a visit to Old Orhei, one of Moldova's most impressive historical and archaeological complexes, set in a spectacular landscape shaped by the Răut River.
Discover a place where nature and history blend into an extraordinary open-air museum. Explore the remains of ancient settlements, learn about the medieval Tatar-Mongol town of Shehr al-Jedid and discover the fascinating ruins of its historic Tatar Baths.
Visit the remarkable rock-hewn monastery carved into the limestone cliffs, where monks once lived and prayed in secluded chambers overlooking the beautiful river valley.
Continue to the traditional village of Butuceni, where time seems to move at a different pace. Admire its authentic Moldovan houses, charming courtyards and traditional rural architecture. Learn about local customs and everyday life while enjoying breathtaking panoramic views of the limestone cliffs and winding Răut River.
Old Orhei is not just a historical destination – it is a place to experience the soul of rural Moldova, discover its traditions and capture some of the country's most spectacular landscapes.
Before we take you to the winery, we invite you to enjoy a traditional Moldovan lunch at a charming countryside guesthouse in Butuceni or Trebujeni village. Be welcomed with warm hospitality and homemade dishes prepared using fresh, locally sourced ingredients, accompanied by delicious homemade wine. Experience the authentic flavors of rural Moldova in a cozy and welcoming atmosphere.

TO CHÂTEAU VARTELY – WHERE WINE TRADITION MEETS INNOVATION
Our journey continues to Château Vartely, a beautiful winery located on the picturesque hills of Orhei, where elegant architecture, stunning landscapes and the art of winemaking come together.
Established in 2008, Château Vartely has become one of Moldova's most recognized wine destinations, combining modern winemaking technologies with a passion for quality and local wine traditions.
During our visit, discover the history of the winery, explore its wine cellars and learn about the winemaking process, from carefully selected grapes to the creation of distinctive wines.
One of the most extraordinary highlights of the visit is The Legend of Wine – a unique immersive visual projection displayed directly onto the oak barrels in the wine cellar. Through spectacular light effects and animated images, the barrels become a giant screen, bringing the legend of winemaking to life and taking visitors on a fascinating visual journey through the origins and traditions of wine.
And, of course, the experience would not be complete without tasting the wines! 
Enjoy a selection of Château Vartely's carefully crafted wines, discover their distinctive aromas and flavors, and appreciate the richness of Moldova's winemaking heritage.
