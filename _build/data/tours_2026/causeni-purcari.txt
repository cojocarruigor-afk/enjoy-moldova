CĂUȘENI – THE MYSTERY OF THE HIDDEN MEDIEVAL CHURCH
Meet your guide and depart from Chișinău towards the south-east of Moldova. Along the way, stop in the town of Căușeni to discover one of the country's most unusual and fascinating historical monuments – the Assumption of the Virgin Mary Church.
Unlike most traditional churches, this remarkable medieval sanctuary is partially built below ground level, creating an unusual architectural appearance that has intrigued visitors for generations.
Dating back to the 17th–18th centuries, the church is particularly famous for its extraordinary interior, entirely adorned with medieval frescoes. The surviving murals, painted in 1763 by artists Stanciul, Radu and Voicu, represent an exceptional example of the late medieval fresco tradition in Moldova, with influences from Byzantine and Wallachian religious art.
Its unusual semi-underground construction is also associated with a local legend dating back to the period of Tatar rule. According to the story, the Tatars permitted Christians to build a church only on the condition that it would not rise higher than a mounted soldier. The builders supposedly found a clever solution by constructing the church partially underground.
Today, the Assumption of the Virgin Mary Church stands as a remarkable symbol of Moldova's spiritual heritage and architectural ingenuity – a hidden treasure well worth discovering.
CHÂTEAU PURCARI – A LEGACY OF LEGENDARY WINES
Continue your journey towards the south-eastern wine region of Moldova and discover Château Purcari, a historic winery whose story began in 1827.
Founded by imperial decree as the first specialized winery in Bessarabia, Purcari has become one of the most recognized names in Moldovan winemaking. Its wines have gained international acclaim, and the winery has received numerous prestigious awards over the years.
During a guided visit, discover the history of the estate, explore its traditional wine cellars and learn about the winemaking techniques that have helped preserve its distinctive character through generations.
The winery is particularly famous for its legendary wines, including Negru de Purcari, Roșu de Purcari and Alb de Purcari. These iconic labels have become an important part of Moldova's winemaking heritage and have earned international recognition over the years.
Discover the character of these iconic wines and learn about the grape varieties, careful production methods and ageing processes behind their distinctive aromas and flavors.
The visit concludes with a wine tasting, offering the opportunity to savor selected Purcari wines and experience the elegance and diversity of one of Moldova's most celebrated wine collections.
