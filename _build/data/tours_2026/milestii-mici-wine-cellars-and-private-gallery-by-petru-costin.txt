TWO GUINNESS WORLD RECORDS: SOVIET HERITAGE & THE UNDERGROUND WINE KINGDOM.
Discover two extraordinary collections, two fascinating stories and two Guinness World Records – all in one unforgettable journey through Moldova!
PETRU COSTIN COLLECTIONS GALLERY – A JOURNEY THROUGH HISTORY
Our morning begins with a visit to the remarkable Petru Costin Collections Gallery in Ialoveni, a unique destination where history, collecting and passion come together.
Step into a fascinating world of historical artifacts and discover an extensive collection reflecting Moldova's past, with a special focus on the Soviet and socialist era. Explore an impressive variety of objects, including statues and busts of historical personalities, sculptures, Soviet and communist flags, banners, military memorabilia, old equipment, coins and other historical artifacts.
Through these objects, symbols and collections discover different chapters of Moldova's history and gain a closer understanding of the Soviet period and its influence on the country's identity.
One of the gallery's most extraordinary attractions is the collection of horseshoes assembled by its founder, Petru Costin, which earned recognition from Guinness World Records as the world's largest horseshoe collection. 
An unusual record and a remarkable example of how a personal passion can become a world-famous achievement!
CONTINUE TO MILEȘTII MICI – THE UNDERGROUND KINGDOM OF WINE
Our journey continues to Mileștii Mici, home to one of the world's most extraordinary underground wine collections and another proud holder of a Guinness World Record.
Prepare to discover a genuine underground city of wine, hidden deep beneath Moldova's limestone hills. The galleries stretch for more than 200 kilometres, with approximately 55 kilometres used for wine production, storage and ageing.
Originally created through limestone extraction, these impressive underground tunnels were transformed into an extraordinary wine kingdom, where millions of bottles rest in naturally cool conditions.
In 2005, the winery's famous Golden Collection, comprising more than 1.5 million bottles, was officially recognized by Guinness World Records as the world's largest wine collection.
During our visit, explore the fascinating underground galleries, discover the secrets of traditional winemaking and learn about the winery's history, its wine collections and the unique conditions that make this subterranean world so special.
And, of course, no journey into Moldova's underground wine kingdom would be complete without a tasting! Enjoy a selection of carefully chosen Moldovan wines and discover the richness, diversity and distinctive character of the country's winemaking heritage.
