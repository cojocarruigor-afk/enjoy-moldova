TRANSNISTRIA – A JOURNEY BACK TO THE SOVIET ERA
This fascinating journey takes you through two remarkable cities, Bender and Tiraspol, revealing their historical landmarks, Soviet monuments, monumental mosaics, local traditions and everyday life. It is an opportunity to explore a little-known corner of Moldova, understand its complicated past and experience a unique blend of history, politics, culture and nostalgia.
MORE THAN A SIGHTSEEING TOUR – A JOURNEY THROUGH A COMPLEX HISTORY
Step into a world unlike anywhere else in Europe! 
Just across the Dniester River, discover Transnistria – a region with a complex history, a distinctive identity and an atmosphere that seems frozen in Soviet times.
Transnistria is a place that raises questions and invites discussion. Its history cannot be understood without exploring the wider story of Moldova, the Soviet Union and the events surrounding the conflict on the Dniester.
Throughout the journey, our guide introduces the historical, political and cultural background of the region, explaining the complex relationship between Moldova and Transnistria, separated by the Dniester River but connected by a shared and often difficult past. This is an experience for curious travelers, history enthusiasts and anyone interested in discovering destinations beyond conventional tourist routes.
VISIT BENDER – FORTRESSES, MONUMENTS AND SOVIET HERITAGE
Our exploration begins in Bender, a city shaped by centuries of history and strategic importance.
We visit the impressive Bender Fortress, a remarkable Ottoman-era stronghold on the banks of the Dniester River. Built in the 16th century, the fortress played an important military and strategic role in the region, witnessing numerous battles and changing empires. Its massive walls and imposing towers tell stories of Ottoman, Russian and other historical influences.
Beyond its medieval heritage, Bender offers another fascinating perspective: its Soviet past. We discover monumental Soviet-era landmarks, including statues of Lenin, and explore the history and symbolism of socialist monumental art.
One of the special features of our tour is the opportunity to discover Soviet mosaics – impressive examples of monumental art that once decorated public buildings and urban spaces. Through their imagery and symbolism, we learn how art was used to communicate ideology, celebrate industrial progress and shape the collective identity of Soviet society.
CONTINUE TO TIRASPOL – THE CAPITAL OF A PLACE THAT EXISTS IN A DIFFERENT REALITY
Cross the Dniester and enter Tiraspol, the capital and administrative centre of the self-proclaimed Pridnestrovian Moldavian Republic, a breakaway region internationally recognized as part of the Republic of Moldova.
Founded in 1792, Tiraspol has a fascinating history closely connected with the Russian Empire, the Soviet Union and the dramatic events that shaped modern Moldova.
During our city tour, we explore its main streets, squares and monuments, discovering the stories of important historical personalities and political figures who influenced the development of the city and the region.
Lenin statues, Soviet architecture, monumental memorials and symbols of a different political era are still part of the urban landscape. Here, Soviet heritage is not simply preserved in museums – it remains visible in everyday life.
 MAKE A STOP THE GREEN MARKET – A TASTE OF LOCAL LIFE
No visit to Tiraspol would be complete without exploring its traditional Green Market.
This is where we get closer to the everyday life of local people, meet local producers and discover regional products, seasonal fruits and vegetables, homemade delicacies and traditional flavors.
We take time to experience the lively atmosphere, interact with local vendors and taste some of the products that make this region special.
ENJOY LUNCH - A CULINARY JOURNEY INTO THE SOVIET PAST
And what would a journey through Transnistria be without experiencing authentic Soviet-style cuisine?
We make a special stop for lunch at a restaurant or café with a Soviet-inspired atmosphere. These establishments are more than just places to eat – they are almost living museums of the Soviet era, where the interior design, decorations, music, traditional dishes and service style bring back the spirit of another time.
Enjoy familiar Soviet-era dishes and discover the culinary traditions that have survived through generations. It is not just lunch – it is part of the cultural experience!
GET YOUR OWN TRANSNISTRIAN SOUVENIR
During the tour, we also make a stop at a local currency exchange office, where you can exchange money into Transnistrian rubles.
Even if you do not plan to purchase souvenirs, keep a few banknotes as a memorable keepsake! The Transnistrian ruble is a unique local currency, issued by the region's de facto authorities and not used outside the region. Its unusual banknotes and coins make a fascinating souvenir from a place that has its own currency, institutions and symbols.
Come with an open mind and a curious spirit – and discover a place where the past is still very much present!
