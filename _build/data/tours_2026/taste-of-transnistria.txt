TASTE OF TRANSNISTRIA – HISTORY, SOVIET HERITAGE & AUTHENTIC LOCAL FLAVOURS
Discover a fascinating region where Soviet-era heritage meets authentic local traditions and exceptional gastronomic experiences. Explore historic cities, iconic monuments and local markets, uncover the secrets of fine Moldovan DIVIN at KVINT, and discover the world of sturgeon and black caviar at AQUATIR. 
A journey filled with history, unexpected encounters and distinctive flavours!
__________________________________________________________
Step into a world unlike anywhere else in Europe! 
Just across the Dniester River, discover Transnistria – a region with a complex history, a distinctive identity and an atmosphere that seems frozen in Soviet times.
Throughout the journey, our guide introduces the historical, political and cultural background of the region, explaining the complex relationship between Moldova and Transnistria, separated by the Dniester River but connected by a shared and often difficult past. This is an experience for curious travelers, history enthusiasts and anyone interested in discovering destinations beyond conventional tourist routes.
First stop is Bender town
Our exploration begins in Bender, a city shaped by centuries of history and strategic importance.
We visit the impressive Bender Fortress, a remarkable Ottoman-era stronghold on the banks of the Dniester River. Built in the 16th century, the fortress played an important military and strategic role in the region, witnessing numerous battles and changing empires. Its massive walls and imposing towers tell stories of Ottoman, Russian and other historical influences.
Continue to Tiraspol
Cross the Dniester and enter Tiraspol, the capital and administrative centre of the self-proclaimed Pridnestrovian Moldavian Republic, a breakaway region internationally recognized as part of the Republic of Moldova.
Founded in 1792, Tiraspol has a fascinating history closely connected with the Russian Empire, the Soviet Union and the dramatic events that shaped modern Moldova.
During our city tour, we explore its main streets, squares and monuments, discovering the stories of important historical personalities and political figures who influenced the development of the city and the region.
Lenin statues, Soviet architecture, monumental memorials and symbols of a different political era are still part of the urban landscape. Here, Soviet heritage is not simply preserved in museums – it remains visible in everyday life.
Visit at AQUATIR 
Prepare for an extraordinary experience at Aquatir, an impressive modern sturgeon farming complex and one of the region's most remarkable production facilities.
Here, we discover the fascinating world of sturgeon – one of the oldest fish species on Earth, often referred to as a living fossil. 
During our visit, we explore the farm and learn about the different stages of the sturgeon's life cycle, from tiny eggs and young fish to fully grown specimens. We discover the characteristics of different species, including Beluga, Russian Sturgeon, Sterlet and Bester, and learn why raising these remarkable fish requires so much time, specialized knowledge and carefully controlled conditions.
And now comes the most anticipated part of the experience – a chance to discover the world of black caviar, often called 'black gold'!
Finally, enjoy a caviar tasting and discover the delicate flavors of this exceptional product, directly from the producer. Experience the difference between varieties and appreciate why authentic sturgeon caviar is considered one of the world's most prestigious gastronomic delicacies.
Soviet lunch experience
We make a special stop for lunch at a restaurant or café with a Soviet-inspired atmosphere. These establishments are more than just places to eat – they are almost living museums of the Soviet era, where the interior design, decorations, music, traditional dishes and service style bring back the spirit of another time.
Enjoy familiar Soviet-era dishes and discover the culinary traditions that have survived through generations. It is not just lunch – it is part of the cultural experience!
Finally, learn the art of Divine making
Discover one of the region's most renowned producers of fine spirits – the legendary KVINT Winery and Distillery, whose history dates back to 1897.
Our visit offers a fascinating introduction to the art of producing DIVIN, Moldova's traditional aged wine distillate, often compared to French cognac. Discover why this exceptional drink has become one of the region's signature products and learn about the craftsmanship, traditions and patience behind every bottle.
One of the most fascinating parts of the experience is discovering the impressive collection of aged spirits. KVINT's cellars hold more than 25,000 oak barrels, with wine distillates aged from 3 to as many as 60 years!
We also get acquainted with some of the distillery's distinguished collections and signature DIVINs, including Nistru, Doina, Tiraspol, Victoria and the prestigious Suvorov and Prince Wittgenstein editions.
And, of course, no visit would be complete without a tasting! 
Enjoy a selection of carefully aged DIVINs, discover their distinctive aromas, complex flavors and elegant character, and experience the authentic spirit of Transnistrian craftsmanship.
This is more than a tasting – it is a journey through over a century of tradition, expertise and passion.
