FOREST & WINE: PLAIUL FAGULUI AND CRAMA MIRCEȘTI
From 4 pax
A refreshing escape into Moldova's wild nature, followed by an exquisite wine experience among picturesque vineyards!
Discover a different side of Moldova through a journey that combines the beauty of nature with the pleasures of wine and gastronomy. Explore the enchanting forests of Plaiul Fagului, discover the richness of local flora and fauna, breathe in the fresh forest air and enjoy a guided nature walk. Then, continue to the charming Crama Mircești winery, where beautiful vineyard landscapes, fine wines and delicious local delicacies await.
PLAIUL FAGULUI – DISCOVER THE NATURAL BEAUTY OF MOLDOVA
Our journey begins at Plaiul Fagului Nature Reserve, one of Moldova's most beautiful protected natural areas, renowned for its picturesque landscapes, rich biodiversity and magnificent beech forests.
Accompanied by a knowledgeable local guide, enjoy a leisurely walk through the forest trails and discover the fascinating natural world of this protected area.
Learn about the diversity of local trees and plants, discover interesting facts about the forest ecosystem and its inhabitants, and get acquainted with the wildlife that makes this natural reserve so special.
Listen to the sounds of nature, breathe in the fresh forest air and take a break from the everyday routine. This peaceful encounter with nature offers a wonderful opportunity to reconnect with the outdoors and discover a side of Moldova that many visitors never get to experience.
CRAMA MIRCEȘTI – FINE WINES, BEAUTIFUL LANDSCAPES & LOCAL FLAVOURS
After enjoying the tranquility of the forest, we continue our journey to Crama Mircești, a charming winery nestled among the picturesque hills and vineyards of Moldova's Codru wine region.
Here, we discover the story of the winery, learn about the local winemaking traditions and explore the characteristics of this distinctive wine-growing area.
Surrounded by beautiful vineyard landscapes, enjoy an introduction to the art of winemaking and discover how the local terroir, grape varieties and winemaking expertise contribute to the character of Crama Mircești wines.
The highlight of our visit is an exquisite wine tasting featuring a carefully selected range of the winery's wines, accompanied by delicious local snacks and gastronomic specialties.
Savor the distinctive aromas and flavors of each wine, enjoy the warm hospitality of the winery and take in the peaceful atmosphere of the Moldovan countryside.
Return to Chisinau in the afternoon for an evening at leisure.
