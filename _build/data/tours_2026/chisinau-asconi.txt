DISCOVER CHIȘINĂU, EXPERIENCE ASCONI – A CITY & WINE JOURNEY
Discover the charm of Moldova’s capital and experience the warmth of its wine-making traditions on a delightful journey combining urban discovery, local gastronomy and authentic countryside hospitality.
Explore Chișinău’s historical landmarks, architectural treasures and vibrant city life with a local guide. Enjoy some free time to discover the flavors of traditional Moldovan cuisine before heading to Asconi Winery, where family winemaking traditions, modern technology and rural hospitality come together.
CHIȘINĂU – A COMPREHENSIVE INTRODUCTION TO THE CAPITAL
Meet your guide at the hotel and set off on a three-hour sightseeing tour of Chișinău, combining comfortable transportation with short walking stops.
More than a sightseeing tour, this is a comprehensive introduction to Chișinău – its history, architecture, cultural heritage and everyday life, revealing the many facets of Moldova’s vibrant capital.
Drive along the city's central avenue, Ștefan cel Mare și Sfânt Boulevard, and discover its main architectural landmarks and historic buildings. Admire the monument dedicated to Stephen the Great and Saint, stroll along the Alley of Classics, an open-air gallery of busts honouring prominent figures of Romanian literature and culture, and discover landmarks such as the Presidential Palace, Mihai Eminescu National Theatre, Organ Hall, National Circus and the historic Chișinău Railway Station.
In the heart of the city, see the historic Bell Tower (Clopotnița), the Nativity of Christ Cathedral, the iconic Triumphal Arch, the Parliament of the Republic of Moldova, the central square bearing the name of Stephen the Great and the elegant City Hall.
Depending on the group's interests and available time, a stop at the National Museum of History of Moldova can also be included, offering a closer look at the country's archaeological and historical heritage.
Experience the local atmosphere at a traditional open-air food market, where colorful stalls showcase fresh produce, seasonal specialities and local delicacies. The tour also includes a visit to Valea Morilor Park, one of Chișinău’s favourite green spaces, known for its picturesque lake and relaxing surroundings.
A TASTE OF CHIȘINĂU – FREE TIME FOR LUNCH
After the city tour, enjoy some free time for lunch and discover the flavors of Moldovan cuisine at your own pace.
Your guide will be happy to recommend local restaurants where you can try traditional dishes, homemade specialties and regional wines. From cozy traditional restaurants to contemporary dining spots, Chișinău offers plenty of options for a delicious culinary experience.
ASCONI WINERY – FAMILY WINEMAKING & MOLDOVAN HOSPITALITY
In the afternoon, leave the city behind and head to Asconi Winery, located in the picturesque village of Puhoi, approximately 40–50 minutes from Chișinău.
Founded in 1994 as a family business, Asconi combines modern winemaking technologies with a genuine respect for local traditions. Set in a charming countryside environment, the winery offers a welcoming atmosphere where wine, food and Moldovan hospitality are equally important.
During a guided tour, discover the winery's production facilities and learn about the different stages of winemaking, from grape processing and fermentation to wine maturation and bottling. Get acquainted with the winery's approach to production and discover the grape varieties and wine styles that make up its collection.
The visit continues with a guided wine tasting, where you will have the opportunity to savor a selection of Asconi wines and discover their aromas, flavors and distinctive character.
The winery also offers a wonderful opportunity to experience traditional Moldovan gastronomy. Its rustic restaurants celebrate local culinary heritage through family recipes, homemade specialties and fresh ingredients, perfectly complemented by Asconi wines.
For those interested in a more hands-on experience, a traditional plăcintă baking workshop can also be arranged, offering a chance to discover one of Moldova's best-loved culinary traditions.
Return to Chișinău after a pleasant day filled with cultural discoveries, local flavors, fine wines and authentic Moldovan hospitality.
