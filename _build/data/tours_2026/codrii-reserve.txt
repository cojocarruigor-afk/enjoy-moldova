NATURE, HONEY & HERITAGE – A JOURNEY THROUGH THE HEART OF MOLDOVA
Discover the natural beauty and authentic traditions of Moldova on a journey through the ancient Codrii forests, the fascinating world of bees and the peaceful surroundings of Hîncu Monastery. Enjoy a guided nature walk, discover the secrets of traditional beekeeping, taste locally produced honey and savour a homemade countryside lunch. 
A delightful day connecting nature, local traditions and spiritual heritage.
CODRII NATURE RESERVE – DISCOVER THE WILD BEAUTY OF MOLDOVA
Our journey begins in the heart of Moldova's Codrii forest region, where we discover the Codrii Nature Reserve, one of the country's most valuable natural areas.
Our first stop is the Nature Museum, located within the scientific reserve. Its exhibition features around 500 specimens of local flora and fauna, offering an introduction to the remarkable biodiversity of this protected area.
The reserve is home to more than 910 plant species and over 1,400 animal species, making it an important natural sanctuary and a remarkable example of biodiversity conservation.
After visiting the museum, enjoy a guided walk through the forest accompanied by a local guide. Discover the richness of the surrounding ecosystem, learn about native trees and plants, and hear fascinating stories about the wildlife inhabiting these forests. Breathe in the fresh air, enjoy the peaceful atmosphere and experience the natural beauty of Moldova's woodland landscapes.
CONACUL MIERII – DISCOVER THE WORLD OF BEES & THE TASTE OF NATURAL HONEY
By midday, continue to the village of Micleușeni and visit Conacul Mierii, a charming honey house nestled among orchards, on a hill surrounded by open countryside, far from the noise and bustle of everyday life.
Here, you will discover the fascinating world of bees and learn about their essential role in nature and the production of honey. The owner will warmly welcome you and introduce you to the property, sharing interesting insights into the life of a bee colony, the tireless work of these remarkable insects and the techniques used by beekeepers to collect honey.
Learn how bees cooperate, how honey is produced and what makes this natural product so valuable. Of course, no visit would be complete without a tasting! Enjoy several varieties of locally produced honey and discover their different aromas and flavors.
The experience continues with a delicious homemade lunch served at the guesthouse. Prepared by the hostess using local ingredients, the meal offers an authentic taste of Moldovan countryside hospitality, with honey-based products and specialties naturally taking their place on the table.
HÎNCU MONASTERY – A PLACE OF SPIRITUAL HERITAGE & TRANQUILLITY
On our way back to Chișinău, make a stop at Hîncu Monastery, a beautiful historic monastic complex nestled among the lush forests of Moldova's Codrii region.
Founded in 1678 by the Moldovan nobleman Mihalcea Hîncu, the monastery is one of the country's historic spiritual landmarks. Originally established as a convent dedicated to Saint Parascheva, it has witnessed centuries of history, overcoming difficult times, periods of decline and eventual revival.
Today, the monastery impresses visitors with its peaceful atmosphere, elegant church buildings, traditional religious architecture and beautifully maintained grounds. Take a leisurely walk through the monastery courtyard, admire its churches and discover the spiritual heritage of this remarkable place.
Surrounded by woodland and far from the noise of the city, Hîncu Monastery offers a moment of peace and reflection – a perfect opportunity to slow down, appreciate the beauty of the surroundings and experience another dimension of Moldova's cultural and spiritual heritage.
After this peaceful visit, return to Chișinău.
