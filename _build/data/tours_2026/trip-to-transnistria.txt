BEYOND THE DNIESTER: TRANSNISTRIA & CASTEL MIMI WINE EXPERIENCE
Two worlds, two stories and a memorable journey through history and wine!
Discover two fascinating sides of Moldova in one day! 
Just across the Dniester River, discover Transnistria – a region with a complex history, a distinctive identity and an atmosphere that seems frozen in Soviet times.
Travel across the Dniester River to explore the historical and cultural heritage of Transnistria, then return to the heart of Moldovan winemaking for an elegant wine experience at the magnificent Castel Mimi.
Throughout the journey, our guide introduces the historical, political and cultural background of the region, explaining the complex relationship between Moldova and Transnistria, separated by the Dniester River but connected by a shared and often difficult past. This is an experience for curious travelers, history enthusiasts and anyone interested in discovering destinations beyond conventional tourist routes.
BENDER & TIRASPOL – A GLIMPSE INTO TRANSNISTRIA
Our journey begins with a visit to Bender Fortress, an impressive historical landmark on the banks of the Dniester River. Discover its centuries-old history, strategic importance and the role it played in the region's turbulent past. Built in the 16th century, the fortress played an important military and strategic role in the region, witnessing numerous battles and changing empires. Its massive walls and imposing towers tell stories of Ottoman, Russian and other historical influences.
Continue to Tiraspol, the capital of the self-proclaimed Pridnestrovian Moldavian Republic. Explore the city's distinctive atmosphere, learn about its history and discover its Soviet heritage. A visit to the traditional Green Market offers an opportunity to experience local everyday life, meet local vendors and discover regional products and flavors.
ENJOY LUNCH - A CULINARY JOURNEY INTO THE SOVIET PAST
We make a stop for lunch at a restaurant or café with a Soviet-inspired atmosphere. These establishments are more than just places to eat – they are almost living museums of the Soviet era, where the interior design, decorations, music, traditional dishes and service style bring back the spirit of another time.
Enjoy familiar Soviet-era dishes and discover the culinary traditions that have survived through generations. It is not just lunch – it is part of the cultural experience!

TO THE WINE CASTLE “CASTEL MIMI” – WHERE HISTORY MEETS THE ART OF WINE
In the afternoon, on our way back to Chișinău, we make a special stop at Castel Mimi, one of Moldova's most beautiful wine estates and a remarkable destination for wine lovers.
Founded in 1893 by Constantin Mimi, the last governor of Bessarabia, the winery combines a fascinating historical legacy with contemporary winemaking excellence. Its magnificent castle, elegant architecture, beautifully landscaped gardens and impressive cellars create an atmosphere where history and wine culture come together.
During our visit, we discover the story of Constantin Mimi and the development of the winery, learn about the winemaking traditions and explore the secrets behind its distinctive wine collections.
The experience continues with a tasting of carefully selected Castel Mimi wines, accompanied by specially paired gourmet snacks. 
Discover the aromas, character and elegance of Moldovan wines while enjoying a refined gastronomic experience in the unique atmosphere of this beautiful wine castle.
