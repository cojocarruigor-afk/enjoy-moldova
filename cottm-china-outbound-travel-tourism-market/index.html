COTTM China Outbound

Home › Blog › Company news › COTTM China Outbound

Solei-Turism will attend the International Exhibition for outbound travel and tourism industry COTTM 2018. The 14th edition of COTTM will be held in Beijing, China, from 16 to 18 April.

China Outbound Travel & Tourism Market (COTTM) is the leading exhibition in China’s outbound tourism industry due to its target audience. Chinese outbound tourism has increased year on year since 2006 by on average 13%.

COTTM is dedicated to B2B and 100% is focused on outbound tourism. The event predicts the trend of the outbound tourism industry and makes a great contribution to China’s outbound tourism industry.

The show attracts representatives of national and regional tourist companies, tour operators and travel agents, representatives of airlines, car rental companies, organizers of cruises, foreign travel, exit tours, Experts of hotel business, insurance companies, suppliers of tourist equipment and accessories, representatives of the press.

Solei-Turism Travel Company, together with some other local travel companies will attend COTTM China for the first time. We do expect to have more customers willing to visit Moldova. We will promote our products and to create the right image of our country.

COTTM attracts over 450 exhibitors from 70 countries and 4300 Chinese outbound tourism operators and travel agents.

  • ITB (Berlin, Germany),
  • Vakantiebeurs (Utrecht, The Netherlands),
  • Tourism Fair (Bucharest, Romania),
  • MITT (Moscow, Russia),
  • COTTM (Beijing, China),
  • JATA Tourism Expo (Tokio, Japan),
  • TT Warsaw (Warsaw,Poland),
  • WTM (London, Great Britain),
  • UITT (Kiev, Ukraine).
