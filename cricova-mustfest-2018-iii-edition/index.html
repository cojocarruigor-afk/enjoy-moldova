Cricova MustFest 2018 III edition

Enjoy Moldova and Cricova Winery invites everyone to celebrate the most grandiose festival of wine and music MustFest 2018 on the 8th and 9th of September.

Get ready to discover a world of fairy tale with knights and princesses, with clowns and magical doors. At MustFest 2018 you will explore, for two days, an adventure in a world full of magic and mystery. Visitors of the 3rd edition will be part of a wonderful fairy tale. This is one the most grandiose music and wine festival in Moldova.

Get your friends and come to MustFest 2018! Experience a colorful story at the most grandiose music and wine festival MustFest 2018 III edition.

Everyone is welcome in the square of the Great National Assembly for positive emotions. A grandiose concert will be held with a live performance offered by following bands and artists: Antonia, Brainstorm, Chaif, Zdob şi Zbub, Alex Calancea Band and others.

The famous Finnish band The Rasmus will conquer for the first time the scene of Moldova at the wine and music festival MustFest 2018 on September 9.

At the event will be illusionists, acrobats, clowns who will create a fairy tale atmosphere, dozens of kingdoms where you will find the true secrets of wine, thousands liters of wine and must, as well as an unforgettable lights show.

Ticket price is 200 lei for one day and 300 lei for two days from July 23 to September 7. Ticket price in the days of the event is 250 lei for one day and 350 lei for two days.

The entry is free of charge for children under 12 years old.

Access to the event is only allowed with the ticket in printed form.

Don’t miss the chance to visit Cricova Winery on MustFest 2018. Only between 8th and 9th of September, you pay just 199 lei for one person for the ‘Must’ package, which includes:

  • Excursion to the Cricova Underground City (Visiting the collection, underground streets, museums, tasting rooms)
  • Tasting 3 samples of wine (2 wines + 1 sparkling wine)
  • Wine tasting is fresh
  • Possibility to purchase “Cricova” products at special price
  • Round trip – return “Chişinău – Cricova – Chişinău”
  • Excursions in Russian, Romanian, English.

Note: The excursions will take place from 09.00 until 16.00, and the transport will be provided from the parking lot of Hotel «Turist».

Tickets can be purchased at Solei Turism Travel Agency –Decebal 64, Botanica, Chisinau. You can call us on 022/890 900 or write us en email on incoming@solei.md.
