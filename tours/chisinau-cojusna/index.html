Discover Chișinău, Taste Moldova: City Tour & Château Cojușna

Full dayPrivate tourLicensed guide

Discover the charm of Moldova’s capital and explore the country’s winemaking traditions on a delightful journey combining history, architecture, local gastronomy and wine experiences.

Your day, stop by stop

  1. 1Chișinău – Discover the Capital

    Meet your guide at the hotel and set off on a three-hour sightseeing tour of Chișinău, combining comfortable transportation with short walking stops.

    Discover the history, architecture and everyday atmosphere of Moldova’s capital as you explore its historic center and main landmarks. Walk along the city's central avenue, Ștefan cel Mare și Sfânt Boulevard, and admire its impressive collection of historic and administrative buildings, elegant façades and cultural landmarks.

    Along the way, see the monument dedicated to Stephen the Great and Saint, stroll along the Alley of Classics, an open-air gallery of busts honoring prominent figures of Romanian literature and culture, and discover important landmarks such as the Presidential Palace, Mihai Eminescu National Theatre, Organ Hall, National Circus and the historic Chișinău Railway Station.

    In the heart of the city, visit the historic Bell Tower (Clopotnița) and the Nativity of Christ Cathedral, admire the iconic Triumphal Arch, and see the Parliament of the Republic of Moldova, the central square bearing the name of Stephen the Great and the elegant City Hall.

    Depending on the group's interests and available time, the tour can also include a stop at the National Museum of History of Moldova, where visitors can discover more about the country's rich historical and archaeological heritage.

    To experience the authentic local atmosphere, visit a traditional open-air food market, where colorful stalls offer a glimpse into Moldova’s fresh produce, seasonal specialties and culinary traditions. The tour also includes a visit to Valea Morilor Park, one of Chișinău’s favorite green spaces, known for its picturesque lake and relaxing surroundings.

  2. 2A Taste of Chișinău – Free Time For Lunch

    After the city tour, enjoy some free time for lunch and discover the flavors of Moldovan cuisine at your own pace.

    Your guide will be happy to recommend local restaurants where you can try traditional dishes and regional wines. From cozy traditional restaurants to contemporary dining spots, Chișinău offers plenty of options for a delicious culinary experience.

  3. 3Château Cojușna – a Journey Into the World of Moldovan Wine

    In the afternoon, leave the city behind and head to Château Cojușna, located approximately 30 minutes from Chișinău. Designed in the style of a traditional French château, this distinctive wine complex offers a fascinating introduction to Moldova’s winemaking traditions and contemporary production.

    During a guided tour, discover the winery’s modern production facilities, explore the atmospheric underground cellars where wines mature in oak barrels, and visit the remarkable Migdal Wine Collection Gallery, home to an impressive collection of approximately 175,000 bottles.

    Continue to the distillery, where you will learn about the production and ageing of Moldovan Divin, a traditional wine distillate matured in oak barrels. The visit also includes the winery’s elegant tasting rooms, decorated in a distinctive medieval style, creating a memorable setting for discovering local wines.

    For an extra highlight, guests are invited to climb the château’s tower and enjoy a beautiful panoramic view over the surrounding vineyards and winery grounds.

    The experience concludes with a classic wine tasting, offering the opportunity to savor a selection of Château Cojușna wines and appreciate the diversity and character of Moldovan winemaking.

    Return to Chișinău after a memorable day combining the cultural charm of the capital with the flavors, traditions and hospitality of Moldovan wine country.

About this tour

This tour offers a wonderful introduction to Moldova and Chișinău – its history, architecture, cultural heritage and everyday life, revealing the many facets of Moldova’s vibrant capital.

Explore the highlights of Chișinău with a local guide, enjoy some free time to discover the flavors of traditional Moldovan cuisine, and spend the afternoon at Château Cojușna, a distinctive winery just a short drive from the capital.
