Țipova Reserve & Fromage Cheese Farm

Full dayPrivate tourLicensed guide

Discover the authentic side of Moldova on a journey combining spectacular natural landscapes, centuries-old spiritual heritage and distinctive rural gastronomy.

Your day, stop by stop

  1. 1Țipova – a Journey Through Ancient Rock Monasteries & Wild Nature

    Our journey begins at the remarkable Țipova Nature Reserve, a spectacular natural area overlooking the Dniester River, where dramatic limestone cliffs, deep ravines and lush vegetation create one of Moldova's most picturesque landscapes.

    Here, we discover the famous Țipova Cave Monastery, one of the oldest and most impressive rock-hewn monastic complexes in Moldova. Carved directly into the limestone cliffs, approximately 200 metres above the Dniester River, the monastery is a remarkable example of religious architecture shaped by nature. Its complex consists of three rock-cut churches, numerous monastic cells and connecting passages, some of which have witnessed centuries of spiritual life.

    After exploring the monastery, embark on an approximately one-hour hike (one way) through the nature reserve towards the beautiful Țipova waterfalls. Follow scenic trails winding through the canyon, surrounded by impressive rock formations, rich vegetation and breathtaking views of the Dniester valley.

    Along the way, take time to admire the area's natural diversity, discover local tree species and enjoy the peaceful sounds of the forest and birdsong. The waterfalls, cascading down the rocky slopes from heights of approximately 10–15 metres, provide a beautiful reward for the walk and a perfect opportunity to pause, enjoy the scenery and capture memorable photographs.

    After this refreshing encounter with nature and history, continue to the nearby village of Lalova, where a warm welcome and an authentic countryside lunch await at a local guesthouse.

  2. 2Lalova – Traditional Moldovan Cuisine & Homemade Spirits

    After exploring the spectacular landscapes of Țipova, continue to the nearby village of Lalova for a warm and authentic countryside experience at Hanul lui Hanganu, a welcoming rural guesthouse known for its delicious homemade cuisine.

    Enjoy a traditional Moldovan lunch prepared with fresh, natural and locally sourced ingredients. Savour authentic regional specialties, including the famous homemade plăcinte – traditional Moldovan pastries prepared according to local recipes.

    But there is more to discover! The guesthouse is also home to a small traditional spirits workshop – a kind of rural laboratory where homemade alcoholic beverages are crafted using fruits and berries grown in the surrounding area.

    Learn about the preparation of traditional fruit brandy (țuică) and discover a variety of homemade fruit and berry infusions, each with its own distinctive aroma and character (at extra charge).

    A delicious and memorable introduction to Moldovan rural gastronomy, local craftsmanship and the generous hospitality of the countryside.

  3. 3Fromage – a Little Taste of France in the Moldovan Countryside

    A little piece of France awaits you in the Moldovan countryside!

    In the village of Horodiște, discover Fromage, a small artisan cheese farm where French cheese making traditions meet the authenticity of rural Moldova.

    Owned by Thierry and Tatiana, this family-run business produces a variety of handcrafted cheeses from fresh, locally sourced goat's, cow's and sheep's milk. Inspired by French cheese making techniques, their selection includes different styles of cheese, some carefully aged for several months to develop their distinctive character, texture and flavor.

    Meet the owners, hear the story behind their passion for artisan cheese making and discover how local ingredients are transformed into delicious cheeses.

    The visit concludes with a tasting of selected Fromage specialties, accompanied by homemade wines – a delightful combination of French-inspired flavors and Moldovan countryside hospitality.

    A day of remarkable landscapes, authentic encounters and delicious discoveries – an invitation to experience Moldova beyond the usual tourist routes.

About this tour

Explore the fascinating rock-hewn monastery of Țipova, hidden among dramatic limestone cliffs overlooking the Dniester River. Enjoy the flavors of traditional Moldovan cuisine in the nearby village of Lalova, famous for its homemade plăcinte and locally crafted fruit spirits. In the afternoon, discover the art of artisan cheese making at Fromage, a countryside cheese producer combining French inspiration with the richness of local milk.
