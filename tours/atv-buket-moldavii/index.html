ATV Adventure & Buket Moldavii Winery

Full dayFrom 4 peopleLicensed guide

Discover a different side of Transnistria on a day filled with outdoor adventure, breathtaking landscapes and authentic local flavors!

Your day, stop by stop

  1. 1Molovata Nouă – An ATV Adventure Through Forests & Panoramic Landscapes

    Our day begins in the picturesque surroundings of Molovata Nouă, where an exciting off-road adventure awaits!

    After a short safety briefing and introduction to the ATV vehicles, set off on a thrilling ride through pine forests, open fields and rugged countryside.

    The route takes you towards a former stone quarry, where you will discover unusual landscapes and explore the surroundings of abandoned underground tunnels and a former mine. Continue across rolling hills and forest trails towards the village of Roghi, enjoying spectacular views over the Dniester River along the way.

    The adventure includes stops at two impressive panoramic viewpoints, where the river opens up to reveal its remarkable width, reaching up to 1.5 km in certain places. Take a moment to admire the scenery, capture some memorable photographs and enjoy the peaceful beauty of this unique landscape.

    ATV adventure details:

    • Duration: approximately 3 hours
    • Distance: 12–36 km, depending on the selected route
    • Terrain: forest trails, fields, hills and off-road sections
    • Highlights: pine forests, former stone quarry, underground mine area and panoramic views of the Dniester River

    After an unforgettable ride, continue your journey with a good dose of adrenaline, fresh-air energy and wonderful memories!

  2. 2Dubăsari – Buket Moldavii: Discover the Art of Local Beverage Production

    In the second half of the day, continue to Dubăsari for a visit to the winery “Bouquet Moldavii”, a local producer of wines and traditional alcoholic beverages.

    Located in Dubăsari, Bouquet Moldavii is one of the region's historic beverage producers, with roots dating back to 1924. Its story reflects almost a century of local winemaking heritage, shaped by the development of Moldova's wine industry during the Soviet era.

    The company's distinctive identity took shape in 1966, when the Dubăsari winery was entrusted with developing high-quality aromatic wines and vermouths. This marked the beginning of the story behind the iconic Bouquet Moldavia brand, created using carefully selected aromatic ingredients and original recipes.

    Over the decades, the producer has expanded its range far beyond wine. Today, its portfolio includes wines, sparkling wines, vermouths, liqueurs, herbal tinctures, balsams, Divin and aromatic herbal teas, combining traditional recipes with modern production processes.

    A visit to Bouquet Moldavia is therefore more than a tasting experience – it is an opportunity to discover a distinctive chapter of the region's industrial and winemaking heritage.

    The guided tour includes a visit to the bottling line, the storage area for aromatic plants used in the production of beverages, and the facilities where Divin – Moldova's traditional aged wine distillate – is stored and matured.

    You will also have the opportunity to explore the intriguing Cosmic Museum, adding an unexpected and distinctive touch to your visit.

    The experience concludes with a tasting of selected products, including wines, liqueurs and traditional infused spirits. The tasting is accompanied by complimentary light snacks and water, allowing you to savor the flavors of the producer's diverse range.

About this tour

This unique experience combines an exciting ATV journey through forests, former quarries and scenic countryside overlooking the Dniester River with a visit to one of the region's well-known beverage producers “Buket Moldavii”.
