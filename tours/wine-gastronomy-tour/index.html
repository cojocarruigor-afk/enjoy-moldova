Highlights

  • The travelers get a genuine taste of the country’s culture and way of life of the Moldovans
  • This tour offers a rich and authentic gastronomic experience and deep connection to wine
  • Warm hospitality by the locals and the rural guest houses is an essential part of this trip
  • Wine routes are always paired with iconic cultural sites
  • Enjoy traditional Moldovan cuisine alongside the elegant wines
  • Enjoy rolling hills, river valleys, scenic vineyards and countryside landscapes, especially around Orheiul Vechi
  • Visit top estates like Château Vartely and the world’s biggest wine cellars Cricova for guided tastings and food pairings
  • A tour offers a deep insight into the Gagausian culture and a blend of Balkan traditions, Turkic language, and rich pastoral culture
  • To truly understand the food and gastronomy, culinary master classes are offered
  • Feel the Moldovan love and hospitality through the food and the portions’ size
  • A trip to a “living museum of Soviet culture,” featuring Lenin statues&Soviet-era architecture- the self-proclaimed breakaway Republic of Transnistria
  • Find yourself in a high-tech industrial facility that represents a blend of a laboratory and a giant aquarium “Aquatir” and witness the “Black Gold” production followed by luxury caviar tasting
  • This tour traces the most significant Soviet heritage in Moldova and highlights “the living museum of USSR”

Day by day

  1. Day 1Arrival in ChișinăuMeals: D

    Transfer to the hotel and a first taste of Moldovan wine at the INVINO oenotheque.

  2. Day 2Chișinău and the wine kingdom of CricovaMeals: B/L/D

    A three-hour city tour, lunch at La Plăcinte, then the Cricova tunnels with a tasting and dinner.

  3. Day 3Old Orhei, Curchi Monastery and Château VartelyMeals: B/L/D

    Old Orhei and Butuceni, lunch at a guest house, Curchi Monastery, then Château Vartely with its light show and dinner at the winery.

  4. Day 4Back to the USSR: TransnistriaMeals: B/L/D

    The Tighina fortress, the KVINT factory with a tasting and lunch, Tiraspol sightseeing and the Aquatir sturgeon farm with caviar tasting.

  5. Day 5Gagauzia, the south of MoldovaMeals: B/L/D

    Comrat, the Besalma museum, a cooking class in Congaz and the Comrat-Vin winery with dinner.

  6. Day 6Farewell to MoldovaMeals: B

    Free time for shopping and transfer to the airport.

Meals: B breakfast · L lunch · D dinner.
