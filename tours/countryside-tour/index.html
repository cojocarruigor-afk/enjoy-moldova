Countryside Tour

Highlights

  • A countryside tour in Moldova offers an authentic and immersive experience
  • Explore villages of Moldova where time moves slowly and customs remain deeply rooted
  • The tour includes walking through villages, visiting small farms, and enjoying scenic viewpoints
  • Discover diverse natural landscapes with rolling hills, limestone cliffs, deep river valleys, forests, and steppe zones within a compact territory
  • Our guests participate in traditional activities such as pastry baking, master classes with glass, artisanal cheese tasting, carpet weaving, wine tasting and other culinary classes
  • The tour focuses on nature, cultural authenticity and a deeper understanding of local life
  • You’ll find yourself in the world’s largest underground wine city Cricova stretching over 120 km
  • Trace the traditional architecture of the villages and stay overnight in rural pensions
  • Witness how the ancient crafts are kept alive and take an active part at some crafts
  • Get familiar with local rural apiaries and taste fresh honey directly from the producer
  • Hike in Codrii Forests and explore one of Eastern Europe’s oldest forest regions, rich in biodiversity, hiking trails, and tranquil woodland scenery
  • Visit peaceful nature sanctuaries like Tipova and Saharna with waterfalls, forest paths, and cliffside monasteries overlooking the river
  • The tour is perfect for those seeking peaceful landscapes, slow travel and authentic experience
