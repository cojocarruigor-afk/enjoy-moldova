### General advice

**Money.** The currency is the Moldovan leu (MDL). Cards are widely accepted in Chișinău; carry some cash in lei for villages, markets and small wineries. Transnistria has its own currency, so ask us before a trip there.

**Time.** Moldova is on Chișinău time, GMT+2.

**Language.** Romanian is the official language and Russian is widely spoken. Your guide speaks English; other languages on request.

**Getting around.** Use arranged transfers or app-based taxis. We can organise your airport transfer and private transport for every tour.

**Safety.** Violent crime against tourists is rare. Keep valuables close in markets and on public transport. Before a trip to Transnistria, read your government's travel advice. → *Is Moldova safe?*

### Passport & visa

**Who needs a visa.** Citizens of the European Union, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Israel, Japan, Switzerland and Norway can enter Moldova without a visa. The full list is on the website of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs: mfa.gov.md → Visa regime for foreigners.

**Your passport** must be valid for at least 3 months after the day you leave Moldova, have at least two blank pages and have been issued in the last 10 years.

**At the border** you may be asked for the purpose of your trip and proof of your stay: a hotel booking or a booked tour, and a return ticket. Your booking confirmation from us helps.

**Other nationalities** apply for a visa online on the official E-Visa portal: evisa.gov.md.

**Entering through Transnistria.** If you arrive from Ukraine through the Transnistrian section of the border, you must declare your entry within 72 hours at a migration office or a Public Services Agency office. We can help you with this.

Links: mfa.gov.md · evisa.gov.md · border.gov.md

### Festivals & holidays (poze cu text scurt — pozele le trimiteți voi)

- **Mărțișor**, 1 March — the start of spring, red-and-white tokens worn for luck.
- **Easter** — the biggest family holiday, with painted eggs and village traditions.
- **National Wine Day / Wine Festival**, first weekend of October, Chișinău — almost every Moldovan winery in one square.
- **Wine Vernissage** — tastings with winemakers, several editions a year.
- **Gustar** (summer) — open-air music and wine festival.
- **MustFest at Cricova** (autumn) — the grape harvest festival.
- **Festivals of traditions**: Duminica Mare, Iprosop (traditional towels), the Carpet Festival in Gagauzia, the Young Craftsman's Festival, Bostaniada (pumpkins), the Medieval Festival in Soroca.
