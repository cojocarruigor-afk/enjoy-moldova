Solei-Turism Diplomas

Home › Blog › Company news › Solei-Turism Diplomas

Dear friends,

We are delighted to inform that the company Solei-Turism has been awarded on 8th April 2016, during the International Exhibition “Tourism. Leisure. Hotels”, according to the nominees for the conquest “Contribution on tourism development in Republic of Moldova 2015”- with first-degree diplomas for:

  • The most representative company (tour operator/ travel agency) for the contribution on the development of domestic tourism.
  • The most representative company (tour operator/ travel agency) for the contribution on the development of inbound tourism.
  • The most representative website for tourism promotion of Republic of Moldova
    Read on moldova-online.travel → Read on moldova.solei.md →

Being founded in 2001 Solei-Turism LTD has become one of the most competitive travel agencies on the travel market in Republic of Moldova.
