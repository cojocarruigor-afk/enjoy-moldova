Enjoy Moldova by Solei Tourism is a Moldovan destination management company (DMC) specializing in sports travel, team logistics and hospitality services for international sports delegations visiting Moldova.

Since 2009, we have been providing comprehensive travel and logistical support for professional football clubs, national teams, youth football teams competing in UEFA youth competitions, beach volleyball teams and other sports delegations.

More about us

Our expertise combines accommodation, catering, transportation, airport assistance, training facility arrangements and on-site coordination, all tailored to the specific requirements of each team.

Whether organizing a football team's away-match visit, accommodating a youth delegation or assisting sports teams participating in competitions in Chișinău and other parts of Moldova, we provide reliable local support from arrival to departure.

We understand the specific requirements of professional football teams and the importance of precision, flexibility and timely coordination.

Complete team travel & logistics

Complete Team Travel & Logistics

One Local Partner. Every Detail Taken Care Of.

Details

We provide comprehensive ground-handling and logistical services for international football teams visiting Moldova.

Our services include:

  • Airport meet-and-greet and arrival coordination.
  • Express Lounge arrangements at Chișinău International Airport, subject to an extra charge.
  • Hotel accommodation and rooming coordination.
  • Full-board catering and individually tailored meal arrangements.
  • Dedicated transportation for teams and delegations.
  • Training facilities and stadium arrangements.
  • Match-day logistics and on-site coordination.
  • 24/7 assistance throughout the team's stay.

From arrival to departure, we coordinate the essential elements of the team's visit through one dedicated local partner.

Accommodation & Catering

Accommodation across Moldova - tailored to the team's needs and schedule.

Details

We offer accommodation solutions in Chișinău and beyond the capital, selecting suitable hotels and accommodation facilities according to each team's sporting schedule, training locations, competition venues and specific requirements.

Whether a team needs to stay in Chișinău or closer to a training facility or competition venue elsewhere in Moldova, we help identify and coordinate the most suitable accommodation options.

Our catering arrangements go beyond standard hotel meal services.

We organize full-board catering and adapt meals to the team's preferences, dietary requirements and sporting schedule.

Our catering services include:

  • Full-board meals throughout the team's stay.
  • Individually tailored menus and dietary arrangements.
  • Meals served at the hotel.
  • Packed meals for away trips and excursions.
  • Pizza, sandwiches and other convenient meal options when required.
  • Food delivery to stadiums and training facilities after training sessions.

We make sure that players receive their meals wherever their schedule requires.

Professional Transportation Services

Safe, Comfortable and Precisely Coordinated

Details

Our dedicated travel and logistics team takes responsibility for organizing and coordinating transportation services for football teams and their delegations.

We cooperate with reliable transportation companies and carefully selected professional drivers experienced in handling transfers for international football events.

  • Modern, comfortable and fully air-conditioned vehicles.
  • Experienced, professional and responsible drivers.
  • Personal airport meet-and-greet.
  • Dedicated transportation for players, coaching staff and management.
  • Transfers between the airport, hotel, stadiums and training facilities.
  • Transportation coordinated according to the team's schedule.

Every transfer is carefully planned to ensure punctuality, comfort and smooth movement throughout the team's stay.

We take care of the journey, so the team can focus on the game.

Training Facilities & Stadium Arrangements

The Right Facilities for Effective Match Preparation

Details

We assist international football teams with arranging and booking suitable training pitches, football grounds and stadiums for additional training sessions ahead of their matches in Moldova.

To help teams make informed decisions, we also provide a comprehensive overview of available facilities, presenting the available options and relevant information about each venue.

  • Booking of training pitches and stadiums.
  • Overview and presentation of available training facilities.
  • Assistance in selecting suitable venues according to the team's requirements.
  • Coordination of training schedules and venue arrangements.

Our local knowledge and direct coordination help teams find suitable facilities for their preparation.

Dedicated Coordination & 24/7 Support

Personal attention throughout the entire stay

Details

At Enjoy Moldova by Solei Tourism, we understand that professional football teams require much more than standard travel arrangements. Every detail must be carefully planned, coordinated and adapted to the team's sporting schedule and specific requirements.

We provide comprehensive operational support, including:

  • Dedicated football team logistics coordinator.
  • Personal assistance from arrival to departure.
  • Continuous communication with team representatives.
  • On-site coordination of services.
  • 24/7 availability and support.
  • Overnight presence in Tiraspol when required.

We believe that reliable service means being there whenever the team needs us.

Our Experience in Tiraspol

Reliable Local Support Beyond Chișinău

Details

Our operational experience extends beyond the capital. We have successfully organized comprehensive ground-handling services for international football clubs travelling to Tiraspol for their away matches.

Among the teams we have assisted over the last years are:

  • Maccabi Tel Aviv FC & Maccabi Haifa – Israel
  • FC St. Gallen – Switzerland
  • Shakhtar Donetsk - Ukraine
  • Dundalk FC – Ireland
  • IF Elfsborg – Sweden
  • NK Olimpija Ljubljana – Slovenia
  • Zira FC – Azerbaijan
  • FK Klaksvik and many others

Our services included accommodation, transportation, catering, match-related logistics and dedicated on-site assistance.

Understanding the specific logistical requirements of teams playing in Tiraspol, we ensure continuous coordination and personal support throughout their stay. When required, our coordinator accompanies the delegation and remains overnight in Tiraspol.
