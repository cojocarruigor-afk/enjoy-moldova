Your team. Your game. Our responsibility.

Enjoy Moldova by Solei Tourism is a Moldovan DMC specialising in sports travel, team logistics and hospitality for international sports delegations. Since 2009 we have supported professional football clubs, national teams, youth teams in UEFA youth competitions, beach volleyball teams and other delegations.

Sports we serve

  • Professional football teamsClubs and national teams visiting Moldova for international competitions and UEFA away matches.
  • Youth football teamsAccommodation and logistics for youth teams in UEFA youth competitions.
  • Beach volleyball teamsAccommodation and logistical support for beach volleyball delegations in Chișinău.
  • Other sports delegationsTailor-made arrangements for teams and delegations of other sports.

Complete team travel & logistics

  • Airport meet-and-greet and arrival coordination
  • Express Lounge at Chișinău International Airport (extra charge)
  • Hotel accommodation and rooming coordination
  • Full-board catering and individually tailored meals
  • Dedicated transport for teams and delegations
  • Training facilities and stadium arrangements
  • Match-day logistics and on-site coordination
  • 24/7 assistance throughout the stay

Accommodation & catering

Hotels in Chișinău and beyond, chosen around the team’s schedule, training locations and competition venues. Meals go beyond standard hotel service:

  • Full-board meals throughout the stay
  • Individually tailored menus and dietary arrangements
  • Meals served at the hotel
  • Packed meals for away trips and excursions
  • Pizza, sandwiches and other quick options when required
  • Food delivered to stadiums and training facilities after training

Transport

  • Modern, comfortable, air-conditioned vehicles
  • Experienced, professional drivers
  • Personal airport meet-and-greet
  • Dedicated transport for players, coaching staff and management
  • Transfers between the airport, hotel, stadiums and training grounds
  • Every transfer planned around the team’s schedule

Training facilities & stadiums

  • Booking of training pitches and stadiums
  • Overview of the available training facilities
  • Help choosing venues for the team’s needs
  • Coordination of training schedules and venues

Dedicated coordination, 24/7

  • A dedicated football team logistics coordinator
  • Personal assistance from arrival to departure
  • Continuous communication with team representatives
  • On-site coordination of services
  • 24/7 availability
  • Overnight presence in Tiraspol when required
