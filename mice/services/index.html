What we arrange for your event

Conference Venue & Meeting Facilities

Selection and booking of conference venues, meeting rooms and event spaces.

Audio-Visual & Technical Support

Sound systems, microphones, projectors, screens, lighting and technical assistance.

Accommodation

Hotel reservations and room allocation for conference participants.

Catering Services

Coffee breaks, business lunches, buffet receptions and gala dinners.

Transportation & Transfers

Airport transfers, hotel-to-venue transportation and group logistics.

Registration & On-Site Assistance

Participant registration, welcome desk, coordination and on-site support.

Interpretation & Translation Services

Multilingual interpreters and translation support for international events.

Conference Materials & Branding

Name badges, printed materials, signage and branded event elements.

Social & Networking Programs

Welcome receptions, networking dinners and evening entertainment.

Pre-and Post-Conference Tours

Guided city tours, winery visits and cultural experiences across Moldova.

Why work with us?

Experience You Can Rely On

Established in 2001, we bring extensive experience in tourism, business travel and destination management.

Local Expertise & Venue Knowledge

We know Moldova's event infrastructure and can recommend suitable venues for conferences, seminars, workshops, business meetings and corporate events, tailored to your group's size, format and requirements.

Reliable Technical Partners

Through our network of trusted local suppliers, we coordinate professional audiovisual equipment, sound systems, lighting, presentation technology and other technical requirements to support the smooth delivery of your event.

One Partner for All Your Needs

From venue selection and accommodation to transportation, catering, technical arrangements and social activities, we bring the essential elements of your event together through one local point of contact.

Tailor-Made Travel Solutions

Every program is designed around your objectives, schedule, group size and budget and adapted to individual travelers, groups and corporate clients.

Quality & Attention to Detail

We value reliability, punctuality, clear communication and careful coordination at every stage.

Beyond the Business Agenda

We enrich corporate programs with wine experiences, gastronomy, cultural discoveries and team-building activities that showcase the best of Moldova.
