Business events in Moldova, organised by a local DMC

Moldova is a welcoming destination for conferences, executive meetings, incentive trips, team building and corporate celebrations. Business sessions in modern conference hotels in central Chișinău can be combined with wine cellars, traditional cuisine and cultural visits that bring participants together beyond the meeting room.

Enjoy Moldova by Solei Tourism has organised travel, business trips and events in Moldova since 2001. We select and book the venue and hotels, arrange transfers, catering, audio-visual equipment, interpreters and the social programme, so your team works with one local point of contact from the first request to the last transfer.

Versatile event venues

From modern conference hotels and professional meeting facilities to atmospheric wineries and unique countryside locations, Moldova offers a variety of settings for different event formats.

Conference hotels in Chișinău

  • Radisson Blu Leogrand 5★: 143 rooms, six function rooms (520 sq m) and Moldova’s Convention Center (1,200 sq m, four divisible sections)
  • Courtyard by Marriott 4★: modern meeting and event space with audio-visual equipment, next to MallDova
  • MIMI Hotel & Business Center 4★: 108 rooms, six conference rooms for 10 to 150 participants
  • Bristol Central Park 4★: 114 rooms, three halls for up to 120, 100 and 30 participants
  • Mercure Chisinau Center Jazz 4★: 74 rooms, three meeting rooms, the largest about 150 sq m for up to 130 participants
