Radisson Blu Leogrand Hotel & Convention Center

6 function rooms + Convention Center · largest: 1,200 sq m

Details

Best for: A leading option for international conferences, corporate meetings, forums, gala dinners and high-level business events. Its central location and full-service hotel facilities make it a strong choice for multi-day programs.

Radisson Blu Leogrand Hotel & Convention Center is a prestigious five-star hotel located in the heart of Chișinău, offering an excellent combination of luxury accommodation, state-of-the-art conference facilities and premium dining experiences. It is an ideal venue for international conferences, corporate meetings, incentive groups, gala dinners and high-profile business events.

Accommodation & Conference Facilities

  • 143 stylishly decorated guest rooms and suites.
  • Six fully equipped function rooms with a total area of 520 sq m.
  • Moldova’s Convention Center, featuring four divisible sections and a total area of 1,200 sq m, suitable for large-scale conferences, exhibitions and corporate events.

Restaurants & Bars

  • Apty’s Pub – a welcoming lobby bar, ideal for informal meetings and networking.
  • Ambassador Restaurant – the hotel’s main all-day dining restaurant.
  • New York Restaurant & Bar – casual American-style dining and bar service, open 24 hours.
  • ZAXI Restaurant – an exclusive rooftop dining venue offering Asian fusion cuisine and an upscale atmosphere.

Courtyard by Marriott Hotel

Meeting & event space · largest: 285 sq m in total (to be confirmed)

Details

Best for: Courtyard by Marriott Chișinău is particularly well suited to corporate groups seeking modern accommodation, professional meeting facilities and convenient access to shopping and leisure amenities. Its international brand standards, dedicated event spaces and on-site restaurant make it a practical choice for business meetings, training sessions, conferences and small to medium-sized corporate events.

Courtyard by Marriott Chișinău is a modern four-star international hotel combining contemporary comfort, functional design and professional business facilities. Conveniently located next to MallDova Shopping Mall, the hotel is an excellent choice for business travelers, corporate groups, conferences and meetings.

As an internationally recognized hotel brand, Courtyard by Marriott offers a comfortable and efficient environment for both business and leisure stays.

Accommodation

  • 118 well-appointed guest rooms designed to combine comfort and functionality for a productive stay.
  • 87 Standard Queen Rooms.
  • 22 additional rooms in other categories, with the remaining room configuration to be confirmed.

Meetings & Events

  • 285 sq m of meeting and event space (to be confirmed; marriott.com lists 4 event rooms, 162 sq m in total, up to 180 guests).
  • Modern audio-visual equipment to support conferences, business meetings, presentations and corporate events.
  • Flexible facilities suitable for a variety of meeting formats.

Restaurant & Dining

  • New York Restaurant – the hotel’s main dining venue, offering breakfast, lunch and dinner.
  • Operating hours: 06:30–00:00.
  • Maximum capacity: 85 guests, making it suitable for group dining and corporate meals.

MIMI Hotel & Business Center

6 conference and event spaces · largest: 182 sq m, 48–150 guests

Details

Best for: MIMI Hotel & Business Center is particularly well suited to corporate groups seeking centrally located accommodation combined with flexible meeting facilities and on-site catering. Its range of conference rooms, modernized guest rooms and dedicated event services makes it a practical choice for business meetings, training programs, seminars and institutional events in Chișinău. The hotel is especially attractive for organizers looking to combine accommodation, meetings and catering within one convenient location.

MIMI Hotel & Business Center is a modern four-star hotel located in the heart of Chișinău, near the city’s main government institutions, business centres and Ștefan cel Mare Central Park. Formerly known as Jolly Alon, the hotel has undergone extensive renovation and now offers contemporary accommodation and updated facilities tailored to business travelers, official delegations and corporate events.

Combining a central location, versatile conference facilities and professional event services, MIMI Hotel is an attractive option for business groups, conferences, seminars and corporate gatherings.

Accommodation

  • 108 guest rooms featuring contemporary interiors and modern amenities.
  • Standard Rooms – approximately 24 sq m, accommodating up to two guests.
  • Superior Rooms – approximately 30 sq m, offering additional space and comfort.
  • Executive Rooms – approximately 35 sq m, featuring a separate living area.
  • Accessible Rooms – specially designed to accommodate guests with reduced mobility.
  • In-room amenities include complimentary Wi-Fi, air conditioning, a work desk, an electronic safe, a refrigerator and tea- and coffee-making facilities.

Meetings & Events

  • Six conference and event spaces designed for business meetings, seminars, conferences and corporate functions.
  • Flexible room configurations accommodating approximately 10 to 150 participants per room, depending on the venue and seating arrangement.
  • Governor Hall – 182 sq m, accommodating 48–150 guests.
  • Pinot Blanc Hall – 120 sq m, accommodating 32–100 guests.
  • Glera Hall – 87 sq m, accommodating 20–80 guests.
  • Pinot Hall – 75 sq m, accommodating 16–60 guests.
  • 9 Muses Hall – 30 sq m, accommodating 12–40 guests.
  • Governor Hall S – 23 sq m, suitable for small meetings of up to 10 participants.
  • Conference equipment includes a projector and screen or TV, wireless microphone, presentation pointer, laptop, flipchart, sound system and complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi.

Restaurant & Catering

  • DIVA Restaurant – the hotel’s on-site restaurant, offering a selection of local and international cuisine in an elegant setting.
  • Coffee Breaks – several catering packages available for conferences, seminars and business meetings.
  • Business Lunches – flexible dining options, including buffet-style service and premium set menus served at the table.
  • Catering arrangements can be tailored to suit different group sizes and event requirements.

Bristol Central Park Hotel

3 conference halls · largest: up to 120 participants

Details

Best for: Bristol Central Park Hotel is particularly well suited to small and medium-sized corporate groups looking for centrally located accommodation combined with practical meeting facilities. Its three conference halls, flexible room layouts and on-site restaurant provide a convenient setting for seminars, workshops, business meetings and corporate dining. With its spacious rooms and easy access to Chișinău’s city centre, the hotel is a valuable option for organizers seeking to combine accommodation, business activities and leisure time in one location.

Bristol Central Park Hotel is a four-star hotel located in the heart of Chișinău, just steps away from the city’s main attractions, Central Park and the Cathedral. Combining contemporary comfort with an elegant atmosphere, the hotel is well suited to business travelers, corporate groups and leisure visitors.

Accommodation

  • 114 guest rooms and suites featuring modern amenities and comfortable interiors.
  • Standard Rooms – approximately 30 sq m, providing additional space and comfort.
  • Deluxe Rooms – approximately 40 sq m, ideal for guests seeking a more spacious stay.
  • Junior Suites – approximately 55 sq m, featuring a separate living area.
  • Ambassador Suites – approximately 70 sq m, offering generous space and a separate bedroom and living area.
  • Guest room amenities include complimentary Wi-Fi, air conditioning, flat-screen TVs and tea- and coffee-making facilities.

Meetings & Events

  • Three conference halls designed for business meetings, seminars, conferences and corporate gatherings.
  • Congress Hall – suitable for events with up to 120 participants.
  • Assembly Hall – suitable for events with up to 100 participants.
  • Business Hall – an intimate meeting space for up to 30 participants.
  • Flexible seating arrangements, including theatre-style, classroom, U-shape and boardroom layouts.
  • Conference equipment includes projectors, screens and audio systems.
  • Coffee breaks, lunches and other catering services are available upon request at an additional cost.

Restaurant & Dining

  • Bristol Central Park Restaurant – the hotel’s main restaurant, offering breakfast and dining options in an elegant setting.
  • LUD’s Pub – a relaxed venue for informal gatherings, socializing and after-meeting drinks.
  • Breakfast with panoramic city views.
  • Catering arrangements can be organized for meetings, seminars and corporate groups.

Mercure Chisinau Center Jazz Hotel

3 meeting rooms · largest: about 150 sq m, up to 130

Details

Best for: Mercure Chisinau Center Jazz Hotel is particularly well suited to small and medium-sized corporate groups seeking centrally located accommodation with practical meeting facilities and a distinctive atmosphere.

Mercure Chisinau Center Jazz Hotel is a four-star international hotel located in the heart of Chișinău. Part of the internationally recognized Accor hotel group, the property combines contemporary comfort with a distinctive jazz-inspired design, creating a welcoming atmosphere for both business and leisure travelers.

With its central location, dedicated meeting facilities, on-site restaurant and wellness amenities, the hotel is a convenient choice for corporate groups, business meetings, seminars and small to medium-sized events.

Accommodation

  • 74 guest rooms, offering comfortable accommodation for business and leisure travelers.
  • Classic Rooms – available with double or twin beds, featuring a functional workspace and essential modern amenities.
  • Superior Rooms – offering additional space, city views and a comfortable working area.
  • Deluxe Suites and Junior Suites – providing more spacious accommodation for guests seeking additional comfort.
  • Privilege and Family Suites – offering flexible accommodation options for families and guests requiring extra space.
  • Guest room amenities include complimentary Wi-Fi, air conditioning, a work desk, a safe, a minibar and tea- and coffee-making facilities.

Meetings & Events

  • Three dedicated meeting rooms for conferences, seminars, business meetings and corporate workshops.
  • The largest meeting room measures approximately 150 sq m.
  • Maximum capacity of up to 130 participants, depending on the room configuration and seating arrangement.
  • Modern presentation equipment, including screens and projectors.
  • Space for coffee breaks and informal networking.
  • Business corner with printing facilities, providing additional convenience for business travelers.

Restaurant & Dining

  • Jazz Restaurant – the hotel’s signature restaurant, offering traditional and seasonal cuisine in a distinctive jazz-inspired setting.
  • Jazz Lobby Bar – a relaxed space for informal meetings, networking and refreshments.
  • Breakfast Buffet – available for hotel guests, with service hours varying between weekdays and weekends.
  • Catering arrangements for meetings and corporate groups can be discussed directly with the hotel.
