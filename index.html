Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How are day trip prices calculated?

Day trips are private, so the price per person depends on group size: the more travellers, the lower the price. Transport, a licensed guide and the listed entry fees and tastings are included.

Is Moldova safe to visit?

Official travel advice asks visitors to take care because of the war in neighbouring Ukraine, and several governments advise against travel to Transnistria. Read our guide, with links to official advice, before you book. Read more →

Can I cancel?

Asking for a quote is free and doesn’t commit you to anything. Cancellation terms for confirmed bookings are in our cancellation policy and in your quote. Read more →

Is pickup included on day trips?

Yes. Your guide and driver pick you up from an agreed location in Chișinău. Just tell us where you’re staying.

How do I get a price for my dates?

Send a short request with your dates and group size. We reply by email with your itinerary and package price. Read more →
