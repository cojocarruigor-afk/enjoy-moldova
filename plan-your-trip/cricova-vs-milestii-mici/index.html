About MoldovaUseful info

Moldova has two famous underground wine cities, both a short drive from Chișinău. Travellers often ask which one to choose. Here’s how they compare, using the facts from our tours.

CricovaMileștii Mici
What it isAn underground wine city: you drive along streets named after winesThe largest underground wine galleries in Moldova
Size120 km of underground streetsMore than 200 km of limestone tunnels (about 55 km in use)
Famous forWine and sparkling wine production, and luxurious tasting hallsThe largest wine collection in the world: about 1.5 million bottles (Guinness record, 2005)
Down there12–14°C and 97–98% humidity all yearBottles rest about 80 m underground; the oldest wine dates from 1969
Our tourCricova Winery & Old OrheiMileștii Mici Wine Cellars & Petru Costin Gallery

Choose Cricova if…

  • you love sparkling wine and want to see how it’s made
  • you’d like elegant tasting halls
  • you want to combine it with the cave monastery of Orheiul Vechi on the same day

Choose Mileștii Mici if…

  • you want to see a Guinness record with your own eyes
  • you’re curious about the Soviet past: the trip includes the Petru Costin Gallery
  • you’d like to add Căpriana, the oldest monastery in Moldova

Why not both?

They complement each other well. Our Culture, Wine & Gastronomy Tour includes both, or ask us to fit both into your stay.
